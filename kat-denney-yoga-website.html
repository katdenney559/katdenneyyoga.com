Meet Kat

Yoga that makes you feel strong.

I'm Kat, a 200-hour certified Power Yoga and Sculpt instructor with a Bachelor of Science in Health Education. I started teaching in 2023 and love creating classes that challenge you, energize you, and remind you just how capable you are.

My goal is simple: build strength, confidence, and connection — on and off the mat.
