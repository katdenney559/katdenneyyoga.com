Meet Kat

Yoga that makes you feel strong.

Kat is a Seacoast-based yoga teacher with a love for movement, music, and making people feel strong in their bodies. An E-RYT 200 yoga teacher with a degree in Health Education, she blends strength, creativity, and a little bit of fun into every class. Her goal is to create a space where you can show up exactly as you are, challenge yourself, build confidence, and leave feeling better than when you walked in. Off the mat, you can usually find Kat by the ocean, soaking up the sun, or hanging with her husband and cats. 🌊
